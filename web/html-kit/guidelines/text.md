# Text

## `LDS-TEXT-INTRO` · An intro is short and secondary

**Kind.** Rule.

**Rule.** An intro sits below the title, runs to at most two lines, and uses
`lmnd-text-body-small-regular` in `content-secondary`. Under a large title it is
`lmnd-kit-large-title__description`, or `lmnd-kit-top-bar__large-subtitle` in a top bar, right under the
title (`LDS-TOP-BAR-DESCRIPTION`); under a sheet title it is `lmnd-kit-sheet__intro`; anywhere else it is
`lmnd-kit-intro`. The same holds for any description or subheading under a heading.

**Why.** An intro is read on the way to the content. Past two lines it becomes the content, pushing what
the screen is actually for below the fold. In `content-primary`, or at a larger body size, it competes
with the title it sits under.

**Exceptions.** None. Longer explanation belongs in the content — a card of list items, a notice, or its
own section — not above it.

**Do / don't.** One or two lines of `lmnd-text-body-small-regular` in `content-secondary` under the
title. Not `lmnd-text-body-medium-regular` or `lmnd-text-body-large-regular`, not a four-line paragraph,
and not a second paragraph beneath the first.
