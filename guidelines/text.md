# Text

## `LDS-TEXT-INTRO` · An intro is short and secondary

**Rule.** An intro sits below the title, runs to at most two lines, and uses `contentSecondary`.

**Why.** An intro is read on the way to the content. Past two lines it becomes the content, pushing
what the screen is actually for below the fold, and in `contentPrimary` it competes with the title.

**Exceptions.** None. Longer explanation belongs in the content — a card of list items, or its own
section — not above it.

**Do / don't.** One or two lines in `contentSecondary` under the title. Not a four-line paragraph, and
not a second paragraph beneath the first.
